# Build brief

## Purpose

Position David Nkemere for GTM strategy and leadership roles by showing how he diagnoses commercial problems, creates a coherent operating model and carries decisions into execution. His creative work is part of the explanation for how he thinks.

The site should feel halfway between a premium web experience and a Bain-style executive deck, with Porsche as a reference for restraint, precision, photography and interaction. Borrow qualities, not branded layouts.

## Visual direction

Generous white space, strong typographic hierarchy, warm white, charcoal, a restrained coral accent, thin rules and large evidence exhibits. Use real project imagery. Small captions establish what each photo represents; KAIRO imagery must never be presented as a portrait of David. Motion should be restrained and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Narrative order

1. Thesis: growth, by design. GTM strategy and creative direction.
2. Net Health: digital performance, organizational scope and the translation layer under the CRO.
3. Seller-led TherapySource test: the bridge into full-funnel work.
4. Account Progression, outbound redesign and how GTM work is commissioned and activated.
5. Northwestern and the creative origin: TESTING, KidSuper, Lil Yachty and Dial Up.
6. California Deep Clean: website, CRM and growth automations.
7. KAIRO: 2026 creative direction, HALO and JWIO for Island / Def Jam / VEVO.
8. Invitation to build the next chapter.

California Deep Clean must remain directly before KAIRO. The nine-slide deck follows the same sequence.

## Interaction vocabulary

The primary experience is a horizontal sequence of nine full-screen chapters, not a vertical landing page. Visitors can follow the sequence using swipe, keyboard, wheel or persistent next/previous controls, or jump through a visual index. Individual chapter hashes support direct linking. Small screens permit internal chapter scrolling when needed; the document itself stays within the viewport.

Photo compositions are navigation surfaces. An archive lightbox, user-controlled inline Dial Up film, and full-screen KAIRO film selector carry the creative work. The GTM chapters include an interactive six-stage Account Progression loop, model tabs, clickable executive exhibits and a 2024/2025 pilot comparison with animated bars. Long narratives and evidence open in accessible dialogs.

Article links open a designed source preview with a direct link. YouTube embeds load only after a visitor requests a film, with a direct YouTube link available. Reduced-motion preferences disable the motion, and offscreen chapters are inert to keep keyboard focus in the visible experience.
